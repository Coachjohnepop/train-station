# Jeremy — The Cool Stuff (10-min test)

**Setup:** Incognito window + hard refresh (Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R) on each page.

Three quick passes — **Prospect**, **Member**, **Coach**. Tap through, note anything that feels off.

## 1. Prospect (the public sell) — ~3 min

1. Open the landing page. **Click or scroll anywhere** → background music kicks in 🎵 (mute toggle is bottom-left).
2. Watch the hero: photos rotate, headline flips "Train with Purpose / Passion / Goals / Commitment."
3. Click **PreSign Up Here**.
4. Answer the **4 quick habit questions**.
5. **Spotlight:** you land on pricing with the right plan **auto-recommended** (Explorer / Member / Pro) and a **RECOMMENDED** badge — it picks the tier for them.

✅ *Cool factor: music + a self-guided funnel that recommends a plan instead of making them guess.*

---

## 2. Member (the client experience) — ~3 min

1. Go to **/member**. Clean dashboard — workouts logged, streak, ranking up top.
2. Open **/member/programs** → pick a day. See **Gym vs Home** options on the same program.
3. Start a workout → tap the **green per-set checks** as you "finish" sets.
4. Back on the dashboard — your **log + strength + streak updated** instantly.
5. Open **/member/chat** — message your coach. Check **/member/today** for the SMS-style daily workout.

✅ *Cool factor: gym/home flexibility, satisfying green checks, and the dashboard reacts live.*

---

## 3. Coach (your control room) — ~4 min

1. Go to **/admin/programs** → open **Adult**. Compact builder, no walls of text.
2. On any day: **+Gym** → **+ex** → add 4-5 exercises from the pill strip. Edit **sets/reps live**, **swap** one, delete one. Use **copy week**.
3. Go to **/admin/today** → assign **John & Steph**, **paste an SMS workout**, **Preview parse**, **Build**.
4. Open **/admin/chat** — **Live/Asynch inbox**, member toggle. **NEW: your chat replies go out as real SMS** to the client.
5. Peek at **/admin/reports** for the member snapshot.

✅ *Cool factor: build a week in seconds, paste-to-program from a text, and chat that texts your clients back.*
